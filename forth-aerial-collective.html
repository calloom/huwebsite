Forth Arts & Aerial Collective

Empowerment through movement and creativity

Forth Aerial Collective is formed of artists working across aerial dance, movement, visual arts and theatre, all with an interest in using aerial and creativity as tools for empowerment, connection and resilience. Our sessions are trauma informed and focus on well-being.

We work with women and non-binary folks in the Forth Valley, projects are open to folks of all ages (18+), sizes and levels of fitness. No prior experience in aerial or other art practices is required. All of our projects are fully funded and we can support participants with travel, childcare or other access costs.

Wingspan

Wingspan is a 12 week multi-art form project for women and non-binary folks who've experienced trauma or poor mental health.

Participants will take part in 12 weeks of workshops, exploring aerial dance, movement, circus, writing, film and visual art. The group will work together to create piece which will be performed to an invited audience.
